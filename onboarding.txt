Influencer Onboarding Questions

1.  Question: Tell us about yourself Field: bio Answer Type: Text input
    Options: Free text

2.  Question: Where are you located? Field: location Answer Type: Text
    input Options: Free text (City / Country)

3.  Question: Upload your profile picture Field: image Answer Type: File
    upload Options: Image file (jpg, png)

4.  Question: What is your primary platform? Field: primaryPlatform
    Answer Type: Single choice Options:

-   Instagram
-   TikTok
-   YouTube
-   Facebook
-   Other

5.  Question: Add your social media links Field: socialMediaLinks Answer
    Type: URL inputs Options:

-   Instagram URL
-   TikTok URL
-   YouTube URL
-   Facebook URL
-   Other

6.  Question: How many followers do you have on your primary platform?
    Field: followersCount Answer Type: Single choice / number Options:

-   0–5K
-   5K–10K
-   10K–50K
-   50K–100K
-   100K+

7.  Question: What is your average engagement rate? Field:
    engagementRate Answer Type: Single choice / number Options:

-   1%
-   2%
-   3%
-   5%
-   10%+

8.  Question: What categories do you create content about? Field:
    categories Answer Type: Multi-select Options:

-   Beauty
-   Fashion
-   Food
-   Fitness
-   Travel
-   Technology
-   Gaming
-   Lifestyle
-   Education

9.  Question: What content formats do you create? Field: contentTypes
    Answer Type: Multi-select Options:

-   Post
-   Story
-   Reel
-   Short video
-   Long-form video
-   Live stream

10. Question: What types of collaborations are you open to? Field:
    collaborationTypes Answer Type: Multi-select Options:

-   Sponsored Post
-   Product Review
-   Brand Ambassador
-   Affiliate Marketing
-   Event Appearance

11. Question: What is your audience age range? Field: audienceAgeRange
    Answer Type: Single choice Options:

-   13–17
-   18–24
-   25–34
-   35–44
-   45+

12. Question: What is your audience gender? Field: audienceGender Answer
    Type: Single choice Options:

-   Mostly Male
-   Mostly Female
-   Mixed

13. Question: Where is most of your audience located? Field:
    audienceLocation Answer Type: Text input Options: Country / Region

14. Question: What are your audience interests? Field: interests Answer
    Type: Multi-select Options:

-   Fashion

    Type: Text input Options: Free text

    Answer Type: Single choice Options:

-   Startup
-   Small Business
-   Brand
-   Agency
-   E-commerce
-   Personal Brand

3.  Question: What industry are you in? Field: industry Answer Type:
    Single choice Options:

-   Fashion
-   Food & Beverage
-   Technology
-   Health & Fitness
-   Beauty & Cosmetics
-   Education
-   Travel & Tourism
-   Gaming
-   Finance
-   Other

4.  Question: Where is your business located? Field: location Answer
    Type: Text input Options: City / Country

5.  Question: Describe your business Field: description Answer Type:
    Text input (long text) Options: Free text

6.  Question: Upload your brand logo or business image Field: image
    Answer Type: File upload Options: Image file (jpg, png)

7.  Question: What is your website URL? Field: website Answer Type: URL
    input Options: Valid website link

8.  Question: What is your business phone number? Field: phoneNumber
    Answer Type: Text input Options: International phone number format

    platformsUsed Answer Type: Multi-select Options:

-   Instagram
-   Facebook
-   TikTok
-   YouTube
-   Google Ads
-   LinkedIn
-   Snapchat
-   Twitter (X)

10. Question: What is your primary marketing goal? Field:
    primaryMarketingGoal Answer Type: Single choice Options:

-   Brand Awareness
-   Lead Generation
-   Increase Sales
-   App Downloads
-   Community Growth
-   Product Launch Promotion

11. Question: Who is your target audience? Field: targetAudience Answer
    Type: Structured input (JSON-like object) Options:

-   Age Range (e.g., 18–24, 25–34, 35–44)
